FinTrack by Techonjo Effective: 25 September 2026 Local-first · SQLite
Overview

Your Data, Your Control

FinTrack stores account and financial data locally on your device using SQLite. No account data is transmitted to or stored on external servers, except where you explicitly use the AI Adviser feature.

You can permanently delete your FinTrack account and all associated local data at any time using the options described below.

Option 1 · In-App Deletion

Delete Account Inside FinTrack

The quickest way to remove your account and all associated data is directly within the app.

  1. Open the FinTrack app on your device.
  2. Navigate to Settings (or your Profile).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

This action permanently removes your account profile and all financial data stored on the device. It cannot be undone.

Option 2 · Device-Level Deletion

Clear App Storage or Uninstall

You can also erase all FinTrack data by using your device's built-in storage management tools.

Clear App Data (Android)

  1. Go to Settings → Apps → FinTrack.
  2. Tap Storage & Cache.
  3. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and confirm.

Uninstall the App

Uninstalling FinTrack from your device removes the app and all locally stored data, including the SQLite database.

Clearing storage or uninstalling the app deletes all local FinTrack data including transactions, budgets, savings, subscriptions, tasks, and account information.

What Gets Deleted

Data Removed Upon Deletion

Any deletion method above removes the following data stored locally on your device:

  • Account profile — name, PIN, biometric settings, and preferences
  • Transactions — all income and expense records
  • Budgets — budget categories and limits
  • Savings goals — all goals and progress data
  • Subscriptions — tracked subscription records
  • Tasks & notes — personal productivity data
  • App settings — notifications, theme, and other preferences
