01 · Introduction

Introduction

Techonjo ("we," "us," "our," or "Techonjo") developed FinTrack ("FinTrack," "the App," or "the Application") as a personal finance and productivity application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information FinTrack handles, how that information is used, where it is processed and stored, the permissions the App may request, and the choices and rights available to you.

We take your privacy seriously, particularly because FinTrack may handle financial information and, when you voluntarily enable the feature, bank transaction SMS messages.

By downloading, installing, or using FinTrack, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and agree to the practices described in it.

02 · Information We Handle

Information We Handle

FinTrack is designed to keep most of your financial information on your device. The information handled by the App can be divided into information you provide directly, information processed automatically on your device, SMS information processed for transaction tracking, and information transmitted when you voluntarily use the AI Adviser.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

Depending on the features you use, FinTrack may allow you to enter or create:

  • Transaction records, including amounts, categories, notes, and dates.
  • Budget limits and categories.
  • Notes and organizer entries.
  • An optional profile photo.
  • App settings and preferences.

This information is used to provide the corresponding finance-management, budgeting, organizer, and personalization features.

2.2 Information Processed Automatically on Your Device

FinTrack may store certain technical or application information locally on your device, including:

  • App installation date.
  • Last synchronization timestamp.
  • Dark-mode preferences.
  • Notification preferences.

The installation date and synchronization information help FinTrack manage SMS scanning and transaction deduplication.

03 · SMS Transaction Tracking

SMS Transaction Tracking

FinTrack provides an optional feature that can automatically detect bank transaction alerts from SMS messages.

3.1 SMS Permission

SMS access is optional. You can use the core finance-management features of FinTrack without enabling SMS transaction tracking.

If you choose to enable SMS Transaction Tracking, FinTrack may request permission to read SMS messages on your device.

3.2 How SMS Data Is Processed

When SMS Transaction Tracking is enabled:

  • FinTrack reads messages only from bank senders you explicitly select.
  • FinTrack processes messages received on or after the date the App was installed.
  • FinTrack analyzes the selected messages locally on your device.
  • FinTrack extracts relevant transaction information such as amounts, dates, and descriptions.
  • Recognized transactions are stored in FinTrack's local database on your device.

3.3 Raw SMS Content

  • Raw SMS message content is processed entirely on your device for the SMS transaction-tracking feature.
  • Raw SMS content is not transmitted to, collected by, or stored by Techonjo or an external server for the purpose of SMS transaction processing.
  • FinTrack does not use SMS access to send messages on your behalf.

3.4 Disabling SMS Tracking

You can disable SMS Transaction Tracking through the App's settings or revoke SMS permissions through your device's system settings. Revoking SMS permission disables the related automatic transaction-import functionality but does not prevent you from using other available features of FinTrack.

04 · AI Adviser

AI Adviser

FinTrack includes an optional AI Adviser that provides personalized financial insights based on information you voluntarily submit through the feature.

When you use the AI Adviser, the following information may be transmitted to a third-party AI service:

  • Your typed message or question.
  • A summary of up to 100 recent transactions.
  • General financial information such as income, expenses, and budget status.

This information is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS and is used to generate the requested AI response. FinTrack does not sell or share this information for advertising purposes.

4.1 AI Services

FinTrack may use:

  • Google Gemini API
  • A custom AI backend hosted on Render and operated by Techonjo.

Third-party services may have their own privacy practices and data-retention policies. Your use of the AI Adviser is subject to the applicable practices of those services.

4.2 AI Adviser Limitations

The AI Adviser is provided for general financial guidance and informational purposes. AI-generated responses may contain errors, omissions, or inaccurate information. You should independently verify important financial information and should not rely solely on AI-generated responses when making significant financial decisions.

05 · Information We Do Not Collect

Information We Do Not Collect

Except where specifically described in this Privacy Policy, Techonjo does not:

  • Collect your name, phone number, email address, or account credentials through FinTrack.
  • Collect your device's contact list.
  • Sell or share your financial data with third parties for advertising or analytics.
  • Use tracking SDKs or advertising libraries.
  • Transmit raw SMS content to a server.
  • Automatically store your local financial records on Techonjo's servers.

The AI Adviser is an exception to the general local-storage model because information required to process an AI request is transmitted to the applicable AI service as described in Section 4.

06 · Local Data Storage

Local Data Storage

FinTrack stores financial information locally on your device using a SQLite database through the sqflite package. This may include:

  • Transactions.
  • Budgets.
  • Notes.
  • Application settings.
  • Recognized transactions imported from eligible SMS messages.

Your local financial information:

  • Remains on your device.
  • Is not automatically backed up to Techonjo's servers.
  • Can be exported by you through the App's PDF or CSV export functionality.
  • May be deleted when you uninstall FinTrack or clear the App's data.

Because this information is stored locally, you are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups using the backup facilities available on your device.

07 · Permissions

Permissions

FinTrack may request the following permissions depending on the features you choose to use:

Permission Purpose Status
READ_SMS / RECEIVE_SMS Detect bank transaction alerts for automatic transaction import Optional
USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT Lock the App using fingerprint or face authentication Optional
CAMERA / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Capture receipts or select images for OCR scanning Optional
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Reschedule notifications after device restart Optional
INTERNET Communicate with the AI Adviser service AI Feature
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Deliver budget alerts and morning briefing notifications Optional

You can revoke permissions at any time through your device's system settings. When you revoke a permission, the feature requiring that permission may no longer function, but other features of FinTrack may remain available.

08 · Notifications

Notifications

FinTrack may generate local notifications, including:

  • Morning briefings summarizing recent spending.
  • Budget alerts when you are approaching or exceeding a spending limit.

These notifications are generated on your device and do not require your financial information to be transmitted to a server for notification generation. You can manage or disable notifications through the App or your device's notification settings.

09 · Biometric Authentication

Biometric Authentication

FinTrack may allow you to protect the App using your device's biometric authentication system. When biometric authentication is enabled:

  • FinTrack uses the biometric authentication facilities provided by your device.
  • Biometric processing is handled by the device's operating system.
  • Techonjo does not access, collect, store, or transmit your fingerprint or facial biometric data.
  • FinTrack uses the Android biometric authentication system to request authentication from the device.
10 · Receipt & OCR Features

Receipt & OCR Features

Where available, FinTrack may allow you to capture receipts or select images for optical character recognition (OCR). The App may use Google ML Kit for on-device OCR processing.

The relevant permission, such as camera or image/storage access, is optional and is only required when you use the corresponding functionality.

11 · Data Retention

Data Retention

11.1 Local Data

Information stored locally on your device remains there until you delete it through the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall FinTrack.

11.2 AI Adviser Information

Information transmitted to AI services when you use the AI Adviser is subject to the applicable retention practices of the relevant AI service and Techonjo's AI backend. Techonjo does not retain AI chat information longer than necessary to process the applicable request, subject to applicable technical and service requirements.

12 · Children's Privacy

Children's Privacy

FinTrack is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child under 13 has used FinTrack and provided personal information, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

13 · Data Security

Data Security

We use appropriate technical measures intended to protect information handled by FinTrack. These measures include:

  • Local storage of financial information on the user's device.
  • The security protections provided by the user's Android device.
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for applicable API communications.
  • Secure handling of application API credentials during the build and deployment process.
  • Android system-level biometric authentication.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any authentication credentials used to protect FinTrack.

Third-Party Services

Your Privacy Rights & Choices
